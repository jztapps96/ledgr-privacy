Ledgr Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Ledgr ("the app") is a workout, cardio and nutrition tracker published by JZT Apps. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Your data stays on your device. We don't have accounts, servers or analytics, and we can't see anything you log.

Information the app stores on your device

To work, the app saves the following in your phone's local storage:

  • Workout routines, logged sets, weights, cardio times and distances
  • Food logs, saved meals, custom foods and water intake
  • Body weight entries, your profile (such as name, sex, height) and nutrition goals
  • App settings and preferences

This information is never sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Information sent to third parties

When you scan a barcode or search for a food, the app sends the barcode number or the search text to the Open Food Facts database to find the product. No account details, personal information or other data from the app is included in these requests. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies to those requests, and they may see your IP address as with any web request.

Camera

Barcode scanning uses Google's on-device code scanner. The camera image is processed on your device and is not stored or sent to us.

Backups and exports

The app can export your data to a file, and can save automatic backups if you turn that on. These files are saved where you choose, on your device or in a place you share them to. We do not receive them. If you share a backup, it is handled by the app you share it with.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy
